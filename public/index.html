Where do I find the right link?
  1. Open Google Ads Transparency Center and search for the brand or its website.
  2. Choose the advertiser’s name to open its advertiser page — not an individual ad.
  3. Copy the link from your browser’s address bar. It should look like https://adstransparency.google.com/advertiser/AR12345678901234567890. Search-result links and creative links won’t work.
